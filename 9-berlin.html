Welcome

You’re joining the first version of this course, and I’m excited to build it with you. After our last class, I’d love your honest feedback: what helped, what didn’t, and what you wish we’d covered. Your experience will help shape what comes next. Thank you for saying yes.

The idea

You keep a short log of what you did each day, five minutes, from your phone. The log shows where you need help most.

Then we build assistants that use it: a daily review that closes your day, a morning brief that tells you what's next, and one agent for the first thing you'd hand to a real assistant.

By the fifth class you can build another agent whenever you need one.

The five days
  1. [Class 1]

    How AI works, and what you’d hand to an assistant

    How AI works, and why answers go wrong. If a real person were your assistant, what would you hand over? You write the list and start your daily log.

  2. [Class 2]

    Your daily log, review and morning brief

    The log becomes a daily review, and a morning brief arrives on your phone.

  3. [Class 3]

    Bring your questions, let’s build together

    We go through the problems of the week. Then time to build, with me in the room.

  4. [Class 4]

    Build your own agent

    I show a content agent for LinkedIn or Substack. You build one for the first thing on your own list.

  5. [Class 5]

    Test and improve what you built

    Run it like a stranger would, see what breaks, and fix it. More time to build.

What you need
  • Claude Pro plan.
  • A place for your log that works on your phone: Google Docs or Notion. We choose together in class 2.
  • A working laptop.
  • Optional: Obsidian, free, to see your notes as a map. Skip it if your laptop doesn't allow installs.
  • You decide what Claude can see. We start with one page and nothing else.
